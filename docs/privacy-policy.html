Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Firefighter Wellness App ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information. The App is designed to support firefighter wellness, fitness, nutrition, readiness, and training workflows.

Information We Collect

The App may collect information that users choose to enter, including:

  • Account information, such as email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information, such as name, goals, experience level, and wellness preferences.
  • Fitness and workout information, such as exercise logs, sets, reps, weights, programs, and progress history.
  • Nutrition information, such as meal logs, macro targets, calorie estimates, and dietary preferences.
  • Wellness and readiness information, such as mood, energy, sleep, hydration, check-ins, body weight, and related progress data.
  • App interaction data needed to operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the App.

How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide workout, nutrition, readiness, and wellness tracking features.
  • Generate personalized plans, summaries, recommendations, and progress charts.
  • Maintain app security, diagnose issues, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or account deletion requests.

AI and Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to operate core features, including Firebase or other Google services for authentication, storage, analytics, and app infrastructure. The App may also use third-party nutrition databases and AI services to estimate meal information, generate wellness guidance, or assist with workout and nutrition planning.

Information sent to these services is used only to provide App functionality, maintain security, support operations, or improve service quality. Users should avoid entering information they do not want processed by these services.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only with service providers that help operate the App, when required by law, to protect users or the App, or with the user's consent.

Health and Wellness Information

The App may process fitness, nutrition, readiness, and wellness information. The App is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical advice. Users should consult a qualified professional before making medical, nutrition, or fitness decisions.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect user information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may request account deletion and deletion of associated user data. Deletion requests will be processed unless retention is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Children

The App is intended for firefighter wellness use and is not directed to children. Users should not use the App if they are under the age required by applicable law or department policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account deletion requests, contact the developer using the contact email listed for Firefighter Wellness App in Google Play Console or the Google Play Store listing.
